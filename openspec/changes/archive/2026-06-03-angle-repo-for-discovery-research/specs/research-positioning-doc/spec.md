## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Preserve discovery research posture
The documentation SHALL instruct future work to generate testable, non-obvious hypotheses in addition to conservative baseline-driven experiments.

#### Scenario: Future agent proposes a discovery experiment
- **WHEN** future work proposes a high-risk or against-the-grain experiment
- **THEN** the documentation requires the hypothesis, mainstream assumption being challenged, falsifier, controls, and expected evidence artifact

### Requirement: Guard against novelty theater
The documentation SHALL distinguish disciplined discovery research from unsupported speculation.

#### Scenario: Reader evaluates a strange idea
- **WHEN** a reader reviews an unconventional research idea
- **THEN** the documentation requires prior-art refresh, measurable predictions, confounder handling, and a clear stop rule before the idea can be treated as progress
